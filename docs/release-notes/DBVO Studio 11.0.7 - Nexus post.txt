[size=5][b][color=#00ffff]New: Proofread lines[/color][/b][/size]
Mod authors aren't always great at spelling. [b][color=#00ffff]Proofread…[/color][/b] in Phase 1 takes you through the lines one at a time:
[list]
[*]The spellchecker underlines typos; right-click a word for suggestions.[/*]
[*]Optional [b][color=#00ffff]AI suggestions[/color][/b] from your AI provider (free ones run on your PC) catch what a spellchecker can't: doubled words, their/there, broken grammar. Each one shows what changes, and nothing changes until you accept it.[/*]
[*]The AI is told to keep each character's voice. Changes that are only style are marked [i]check before accepting[/i], so a Khajiit keeps speaking like one.[/*]
[*]Suggestions follow the DBVO script's rules: no semicolons, brackets or curly quotes that would break a line.[/*]
[*]Game markup such as <Alias=Player> and (Persuade) is locked, so it can't be broken by accident.[/*]
[*]Every edit goes into the DBVO Fix plugin, and you can stop any time and pick up where you left off.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#00ffff]A friendlier FOMOD builder[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Pages anywhere:[/b] + Page above and + Page below, or right-click a page in the tree. Right-click a group for + Group above or below.[/*]
[*][b]Moving things works:[/b] clicking ↑ twice no longer moves a page back where it was. ⤒ and ⤓ jump to the top or bottom, and pages and groups can be dragged.[/*]
[*][b]Page and group order:[/b] many imported FOMODs sort pages A–Z in the installer, whatever order you set. You can now see and change that, and moving a page switches it to your own order.[/*]
[*][b]Add DBVO Patches[/b] starts with nothing ticked, with a search box and "Tick new ones", so adding to an imported FOMOD doesn't mean unticking everything.[/*]
[*][b]Ready-made option descriptions:[/b] the mod's name, its number of lines, the DBVO Fix notice when needed, the mod page and the version. Add the mod page when you create a mod in Phase 1.[/*]
[*][b]Your edits stay in DBVO Studio[/b] until you build; the app now says so. [b]Build without packing[/b] writes the finished installer as a folder next to the archive, ready to zip with 7-Zip yourself if you prefer.[/*]
[*][b]Linked options respect your edits:[/b] a file you remove stays removed, and a DBVO Fix plugin that doesn't exist is no longer put back at every build.[/*]
[*]No second voice pack JSON when your installer already installs one.[/*]
[*]A progress bar from the moment you click Build, and a "Working…" spinner whenever the app needs a moment, so it never looks frozen.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#00ffff]Fixes worth knowing about[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]BSA archives:[/b] names with ’ “ ” … – — were stored as "?", so the game never found those files. They're stored correctly now; rebuild the FOMOD, no new generation needed.[/*]
[*]AI Auto Tag: accept or undo all suggestions at once instead of one line at a time. Newer Claude models work for AI Auto Tag and Proofread.[/*]
[*]Right after typing in a list, a click on a button such as ★, ⚑ or ♪ sometimes needed a second click. One is enough now.[/*]
[*]Clicking regenerate on several lines quickly could leave a ↻ spinning for good, stop one too early, or lose a take.[/*]
[*]Changing folders, options or Books part settings while a project generated could drop takes you paid for. Those changes now wait for the generation to end.[/*]
[*]FOMOD import keeps more of the original (header image settings, file flags, older text encodings), and two outside folders with the same name no longer mix their files.[/*]
[*]DBReV packs (BETA TESTING): reference sets no longer leave lines out.[/*]
[*]OpenAI-compatible: your OpenAI key is never sent to a server on your PC or network, and pasting the full endpoint address works.[/*]
[*]Plus lots of smaller fixes: see the changelog.[/*]
[/list]

The user guide (F1) and the PDF cover all of it. As always, DM me or find me on the DBVO Discord if something breaks.
