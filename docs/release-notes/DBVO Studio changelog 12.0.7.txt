Version 12.0.7 (changes since 11.0.10)
- New: Variants. ★ Variants on the Phase 2 patch list opens a page per voice pack with every line of all its patches. A line with variants plays a different take each time you say it in game (Dragonborn Voice Over 2 and Dragonborn ReVoiced pick one at random). A line with the same file name in several patches is one line on the page.
- Only lines your patches export count on the Variants page. Lines a reference covers (such as Skyrim Base Game), excluded lines and, when a patch exports approved lines only, lines not approved are left out, along with their takes.
- Suggested lists lines that are the same in two or more mods and have no variants yet, most used first. Click a line to hear its takes, star the ones that should play, and delete the ones you don't want with the bin. The take in use can only be changed in Phase 2.
- Generate variants: click a line (Ctrl+click or Shift+click for several) and make one take per row of the Variant settings. There are no ticks to forget: it works on the lines you selected, and always shows those lines and the credits and asks before anything is made. Each row has its own Stability (Creative, Natural or Robust on eleven_v3/v4), Speed, a word merged into the prefix tag, and a seed (new each time, or fixed to repeat a reading). Sent as shows the exact text. Presets: Subtle, Lively, Moods, and your own, which can be replaced or deleted. Changing a row shows Custom.
- A line that has no take in its patch yet, or whose take in use says older words, is skipped when generating variants, with the reason in the message. Generate it in Phase 2 first.
- Variant settings fold to one line that sums up the rows (remembered per voice pack), and their rows scroll inside the panel, so the list of lines always keeps its room.
- Prefix [tags] for variants, saved per voice pack, for example [British Extremely Baleful]. It is used only for variant takes made on the Variants page. Your lines and other takes stay as they are.
- Context on / off shows how the prefix is sent: as context (not billed, a little weaker) or in the text (billed, full strength). It follows your Global Settings default until you change it on the Variants page. eleven_v3 and engines other than ElevenLabs take no context, so it is off there and the credit estimate counts the prefix.
- Takes made with Creative stability can drift out of the voice's accent, so they are marked listen and show under Needs a look until you play them. Playing a take clears the mark; it only becomes a variant if you star it. Purge unused takes in Phase 2 keeps takes not played yet.
- With engines other than ElevenLabs, Stability, Speed and Seed don't apply, and the page says so.
- + Add a line (voice packs with Legacy names): type a line as it appears in game. It shows the file names, checks the line with the Phase 1 rules and suggests matching lines from your mods while you type. Remove this line deletes a line you added, with its takes.
- Variants Pack (Legacy names): every line with two or more ★ takes goes into one BSA, as DBVO 2 numbered files and DBReV L name(1) files (DBReV L: Dragonborn ReVoiced playing a DBVO 1 Legacy pack), with the empty light plugin that loads it and a list of the lines. Both names of a variant share one copy of its audio.A line whose take files are missing, whose name is too long for a BSA, or whose file name is already another line's is left out and listed, and the rest is still built.
- Add to a FOMOD project puts the Variants Pack in a pre-ticked option that users can untick. After a rename, adding it again updates the same option. Remove the Variants Pack deletes its files and takes its option out of your FOMOD projects; your ★ takes stay.
- Folder names that end in a period (a line like "Falkreath.") are kept in the BSA, where the game finds them. Windows can't create such folders, so the Variants Pack has to stay a BSA: unpacking it or repacking it with Cathedral Assets Optimizer breaks those variants.
- With DBVO 2.0 names, each patch exports its own ★ takes, so the Variants page counts the stars of the patch that gets new variants. One ★ there is the line's file in place of the take in use, and the page says so.
- While the Variants Pack is being built, the stars and takes of that voice pack can't be changed, in Phase 2 either, so the pack holds exactly what you starred.
- A cancelled or failed BSA build leaves the earlier archive as it was, with no half-written file next to it.
- Reading plugins is faster and uses less memory: the parts of a plugin a task doesn't need (most of a large master) are skipped instead of loaded. A damaged end in a part no task reads is passed over.
- DBVO Fix plugins are written next to the old file and then swapped in, so a crash or a full disk can't leave a cut-off plugin behind.
- NPC Revoice and the Voiced Books tool use less memory on large load orders.
- Deleting a take in Phase 2 also deletes its kept dry original.
- A voice pack with lines added on its Variants page can't be deleted until those lines are removed.
- Patches made before 9.0.0 still send leading [tags] in the text, where ElevenLabs bills them as characters. When the Studio starts it lists any such ElevenLabs patches and offers Turn on for all or Keep as they are. It asks once per patch, and never about a patch you switched off yourself.
- Phase 2: the Take column is now Takes, and the title over the line buttons, Take creation, sorts the lines by when the take in use was made (oldest or newest first).
- Fixed: a .fuz sub-folder typed as \Sound\DBVO\Vampira\ was treated as a folder elsewhere and replaced with Sound\DBVO\{packId}. It now counts as a folder inside the final folder.
