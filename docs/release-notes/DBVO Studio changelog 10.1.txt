Version 10.1.0
- New: Proofread lines in Phase 1. Proofread… goes through the lines one at a time so you can fix spelling and grammar, with the spellchecker (right-click for suggestions) and optional AI suggestions from your AI provider, 50 lines per request ahead of where you are. Each suggestion shows what changes and why; style-only changes are marked "check before accepting". Game markup such as <Alias=Player> and (Persuade) is locked and never sent to the AI. Saved edits go into the DBVO Fix plugin, and a summary shows what you changed.
- Clicks: right after typing in a list (the spoken text, the in-game line), the next click on a button such as ★, ⚑, ♪ or ⊘ sometimes needed a second click. One is enough now.
- The spinning ↻: clicking regenerate on several lines quickly could leave a ↻ spinning after the take arrived, stop one too early, or lose one of the takes. Each ↻ now follows its own request, also when it waits in the queue or is removed from it.
- Changing folders, options or Books part settings while that project generates could drop takes you paid for. Those changes now wait for the generation; Books text you edit meanwhile is saved when it ends.
- DBReV packs: reference sets no longer leave lines out (DBReV never plays another pack's file for the same words), text variants of one line no longer pick up each other's existing files, and Phase 2 says when kept takes go past DBReV's 20 files per line.
- OpenAI-compatible: your OpenAI key is never sent to a server on your PC or network, a full endpoint address (…/v1/audio/speech) works, and long Books parts get more time.
- Player Grunts' Whisper, NPC Revoice's original audio and .fuz previews no longer ask for FaceFX and FonixData, which they don't use.
- Also: saves no longer fail when another part of the app is reading the same file, the library can't be moved into a folder inside itself, a FOMOD deleted during a build stays deleted, NPC Revoice and Voiced Books scans can be cancelled, lines left "Generating" after closing the app mid-batch are reset, takes with # or % in their names play, and player-name lines export to your real Downloads folder (also when it was moved).
- User guide and PDF: a new section on Proofread lines, what the DBVO Fix plugin copies from a mod (with a note on load order), and DBReV packs with reference sets.
